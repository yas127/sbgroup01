## 🎨 Visual Design System (Replicating Design Template)

* **Background Palette**: Smooth, full-bleed gradient transitioning from coral-pink (`#EE6677`) through peach-orange (`#F28C6E`) into purple (`#8A5BA8`) and deep oceanic blue (`#1B4A94` to `#123A78`).
* **3D Liquid Blob**: Floating SVG organic wave behind the hero content with layered specular highlights, glossy gradients, and subtle floating animation (`@keyframes floatBlob`).
* **Left Vertical Rail**: Translucent dark navigation bar (`#121218`) with rotated vertical labels (`GROUP 01 // SIXFOLD`, `01 QUALITATIVE`, `02 QUANTITATIVE`, `03 FINANCIAL`), peach divider lines, and active status indicators. Collapses into a hamburger menu on mobile.
* **Typography**: Condensed bold uppercase titles (`Bebas Neue` / `Barlow Condensed`) with two-tone header styling (first word lavender `#9D8CFF`, second word white `#FFFFFF`). Body text in clean, legible `Inter` / `Poppins`.
* **Outlined Ghost Numerals**: Large stroke numerals (`01`, `02`, `03`) anchoring section carousels.
* **Dual-Card Carousel**: Featured dark card (`#1E1E1E`) and peach card (`#F28C6E`) pairing with circular arrow navigation buttons (`<` and `>`).
* **Coral CTA Buttons**: Rectangular `#FF6B6B` buttons with uppercase tracking (`EXPLORE REPORT`).

## 📑 Complete 3-Part Research Structure (All 62 Subsections)

### Part 01: Qualitative Research (pp. 4–129 · 24 Subsections)
* **C-Suite Profiles**: Managing Director/CEO **Kirti Ganorkar**, CFO **Jayashree Satagopan**, and COO **Aalok Shanghvi** (analyzing tenure, career background, core strengths, weaknesses, and public sentiment).
* **Company Foundations**: History (from 1983 Vapi psychiatric lab to US$6.36B global powerhouse), Products & Services, Catalysts & Drivers, Cyclical/Secular Trends, Target Market, Competition, Production & Distribution (43 manufacturing plants), Suppliers & Components, Commodities Exposure, R&D ($1.42B specialty pivot), Intellectual Property, Lawsuits, Ethics, Customer Service, HR Recruiting & Retention, Sales Strategies, Culture/Innovation.
* **Visual Strategic Frameworks**:
  * **SWOT 2×2 Matrix**: Internal Strengths/Weaknesses and External Opportunities/Threats.
  * **Porter's 5 Forces**: Interactive hub-and-spoke diagram (High Rivalry, Low Entrant Threat, Moderate Buyer Power, Low Supplier Power, Low Substitute Threat).
  * **BCG Growth-Share Matrix**: Stars (Specialty Biologics: Ilumya, Cequa), Cash Cows (India Formulations & US Generics), Question Marks (GLP-1 GL0034), Dogs (Merchant APIs).
  * **Product Lifecycle**: 5-stage curve from R&D through Rapid Growth to Maturity & Extension.
  * **M&A History & Corporate Strategy**: Ranbaxy, Concert, DUSA, and Taro transactions.

### Part 02: Quantitative Research (pp. 130–164 · 19 Subsections)
* **Market Metrics**: Total Addressable Market (TAM), Market Growth, Domestic Formulation Market Share (7.90% lead vs top 10 competitors).
* **Segmental Realization**: Geographic breakdown (India 33.1%, US 28.6%, Emerging Markets 19.0%, Rest of World 14.6%, API 4.7%), Vertical & Product Exposure, Foreign Exchange (FX) Exposure, ASP & Volume, Cost of Production, Inventory cycles.
* **Operational & Shareholder Metrics**: LTVC, CAC, Customer Concentration, Shareholder Concentration (Promoter Group 52.68% vs. Public 47.32%), 47,000 Employees worldwide, Insider Trading disclosures, Global GDP impact, DAU/MAU in pharma, and Social Media reach (LinkedIn 2.46M+ followers).

### Part 03: Financial Research (pp. 165–195+ · 19 Subsections)
* **Valuation & Guidance**: Stock Details, Guidance conservatism vs. actual results (average beat of +2.9pp).
* **Historical Financials & Peer Benchmarks**: 10-year Revenue Growth (FY16 ₹284B to FY26 ₹582B), EPS Trajectory, Gross Margins (Sun Pharma 76.5% vs. peers), Operating Margins (27.8%), Valuation Multiples (P/E 36.73x, PEG, P/R / P/S 7.0x–7.2x) benchmarked against Dr. Reddy's, Cipla, Lupin, Torrent, and Mankind.
* **Financial Forensics**: Recurring Q3-to-Q4 Seasonality cycle, Accounting Red Flags analysis, and C-Suite succession history.

## 🔍 Data-Quality Corrections & Audit Trail

1. **Reliance &rarr; Sun Pharma Normalization**: 7 section titles in Part 03 of the source PDF originally contained the word "Reliance" due to an inherited formatting template. These were corrected to "Sun Pharma" (*Historical Revenue Growth*, *Historical E.P.S. Growth*, *Gross Margin %*, *Operating Margin %*, *Valuation on P/E & PEG*, *Valuation on P/R*, *Seasonality*). All underlying data relates strictly to Sun Pharma.
2. **Decoded Mathematical Formulas**: Garbled equation glyphs on pp. 131 and 135 (e.g. US generic TAM, psoriasis global market size, and geographic revenue figures) were cross-checked with the rendered PDF layouts and restored to verified numeric values.
3. **Audited Transparency**: A dedicated **Data-Quality Audit Modal** is accessible throughout the site detailing every single correction.

## 🚀 Run & Deploy Instructions

### Prerequisites
* Node.js v18+ or v20+ / v24+
* npm or pnpm

### Running Locally
```bash
# 1. Navigate to the project directory
cd "C:\Users\Yajat Srivastava\iisproject\website"
# (or the active workspace in scratch/sun-pharma-research)

# 2. Install dependencies (if needed)
npm install

# 3. Start development server
npm run dev

# 4. Open in browser
# http://localhost:5173
```

### Building for Production
```bash
# Build production bundle
npm run build

# Preview production build locally
npm run preview -- --port 4173
```

The production bundle is generated in the `dist/` directory and can be deployed directly to Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, AWS S3, or any static hosting service.
